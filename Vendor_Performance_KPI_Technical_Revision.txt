1. KPI CHEAT SHEET
============================================================

Total Sales
-----------
SUM(TotalSalesDollars)

Meaning:
Total sales value.

Business use:
Measures sales scale and is the denominator for margin calculations.


Total Purchase
--------------
SUM(TotalPurchaseDollars)

Meaning:
Total purchasing spend.

Business use:
Shows how much was spent to acquire inventory/products.


Gross Profit
------------
Total Sales - Total Purchase

Project-level interpretation:
Money left after the purchase-cost component used in this analysis.

Business use:
Profitability before other operating costs.


Gross Margin %
--------------
Gross Profit / Total Sales × 100

Best business-level calculation:
SUM(GrossProfit) / SUM(TotalSalesDollars)

Why:
A weighted business-level margin is more meaningful than simply averaging
row-level percentages.


Unsold Capital
--------------
(Purchase Quantity - Sales Quantity) × Purchase Price

Meaning:
Estimated purchase-cost value tied to quantity not sold.

Business problem:
Capital may be tied up in slow-moving inventory.


Stock Turnover
--------------
Sales Quantity / Purchase Quantity

Meaning:
Sales quantity relative to purchased quantity.

Project threshold:
< 1 = low stock turnover.

Important:
This is the project's simplified quantity-based metric, not necessarily a
full accounting inventory-turn formula.


Sales-to-Purchase Ratio
-----------------------
Sales Dollars / Purchase Dollars

Meaning:
Sales value generated relative to purchase spend.


Profit Margin
-------------
Gross Profit / Sales × 100

Important:
If Sales = 0, a percentage can become undefined/extreme.
That is why the EDA observed extreme negative/infinite values before filtering.

10. POWER BI / DAX QUESTIONS
============================================================

Q: Why use a measure for Gross Margin %?

A:
Because the business-level margin should respond correctly to filter context and
should be calculated from aggregate gross profit and aggregate sales.

Q: Why not SUM a percentage column?

A:
Percentages should not be added across rows.

Q: What is DAX?

A:
A calculation language used in Power BI for measures, calculated columns and
calculated tables.

Q: Calculated column vs measure?

A:
A calculated column is evaluated row by row and stored in the model.
A measure is calculated dynamically based on filter context.

Q: Why is Gross Margin % better as a measure?

A:
Because it should change correctly when the user filters vendors, products or
other dimensions.

Gross Profit as a percentage of sales.

Sales quantity divided by purchase quantity in this project.
