============================================================
1. 60-SECOND PROJECT EXPLANATION
============================================================

"I worked on a Vendor Performance and Inventory Analytics project using MySQL,
Python, and Power BI. The objective was to understand vendor sales and
purchasing performance, profitability, inventory turnover, unsold capital,
product performance, and supplier concentration.

I first loaded the raw datasets into MySQL and used SQL for database-level
aggregation and analysis. I then used Python and Pandas for data validation,
EDA, correlation analysis, and statistical testing. After that, I created a
summarized analytical dataset and built a Power BI report using DAX.

The analysis identified low-sales high-margin brands, concentrated purchasing
among the top vendors, bulk-purchasing cost differences, slow-moving inventory,
and statistically different profit-margin behavior between vendor groups.

The final goal was to help management decide where to improve pricing,
purchasing, inventory, and vendor strategy."


============================================================
2. BUSINESS PROBLEM
============================================================

The business needs to control profitability and working capital while
maintaining strong vendor and product performance.

The project focuses on five main problems:

1. Identify low-sales, high-margin brands that may need pricing or promotional
   attention.

2. Identify top vendors contributing to sales and gross profit.

3. Understand whether bulk purchasing is associated with lower unit cost.

4. Identify low inventory turnover and capital tied up in unsold inventory.

5. Compare profitability behavior between high-performing and low-performing
   vendor groups.

5. GRAIN — VERY IMPORTANT
============================================================

Grain means what one row represents.

begin_inventory:
One row represents an inventory record.

end_inventory:
One row represents an ending inventory record.

purchases:
One row represents a purchase transaction/item record.

sales:
One row represents a sales record.

vendor_invoice:
One row represents an invoice record.

purchase_prices:
One row represents a product/vendor pricing record.

Why this matters:

A table can have repeated VendorNumber, Brand, InventoryId, or PONumber values
without being a duplicate row.

Never say:
"Same vendor appears many times, so I removed duplicates."

Instead:
"I checked duplicates at the appropriate business grain."

11. MAIN KPIs
============================================================

TOTAL SALES
-----------
Sum of TotalSalesDollars.

Question:
How much sales value was generated?

TOTAL PURCHASE
--------------
Sum of TotalPurchaseDollars.

Question:
How much was spent on purchases?

GROSS PROFIT
------------
Sales - Purchase Cost.

Question:
How much gross profit remains after purchase cost?

GROSS MARGIN %
--------------
Gross Profit / Sales × 100.

Question:
What proportion of sales value remains as gross profit?

UNSOLD CAPITAL
--------------
(Purchased Quantity - Sales Quantity) × Purchase Price

Question:
How much purchase-cost capital is associated with quantity that has not sold,
based on the project calculation?

STOCK TURNOVER
--------------
Sales Quantity / Purchase Quantity

Question:
How quickly is purchased quantity moving relative to purchased quantity?

SALES-TO-PURCHASE RATIO
-----------------------
Sales Dollars / Purchase Dollars

Question:
How much sales value is generated relative to purchase spend?


============================================================
12. IMPORTANT POWER BI / DAX LOGIC
============================================================

Gross Margin %:

DIVIDE(
    SUM('vendor vendor_sales_summary'[GrossProfit]),
    SUM('vendor vendor_sales_summary'[TotalSalesDollars])
)

Why this is better than simply averaging row-level profit percentages:

A simple average gives every row equal weight. A business-level margin should
be based on total gross profit relative to total sales.

Unsold Capital:

(
    TotalPurchaseQuantity - TotalSalesQuantity
)
× PurchasePrice


TARGET BRAND
------------

A product/brand is flagged as "Yes" when:

- Total Sales is at or below the 15th percentile.
- Average Profit Margin is at or above the 85th percentile.

Business meaning:

Low sales + high margin may indicate an opportunity to improve volume without
immediately sacrificing profitability.


LOW-PERFORMING VENDOR
---------------------
StockTurnover < 1

Business meaning:

The project uses this threshold to identify vendors associated with slower
inventory movement.

============================================================
18. HYPOTHESIS TESTING
============================================================

Null hypothesis H0:
There is no significant difference in profit margins between top- and
low-performing vendor groups.

Alternative hypothesis H1:
There is a significant difference in profit margins between the two groups.

Reported result:
Reject H0.

Meaning:
The analysis found evidence that the groups have different profit-margin
behavior.

Interview answer:

"I used hypothesis testing to check whether the observed margin difference
between the vendor groups could reasonably be explained by sampling variation.
The reported test rejected the null hypothesis, so the analysis supports a
difference in the groups' profit-margin behavior."

34. LIMITATIONS
============================================================

1. The dataset is observational.
2. Correlation does not prove causation.
3. Bulk purchasing findings should not be presented as causal without an
   experiment or stronger design.
4. Missing approval information limits analysis of approval behavior.
5. Inventory turnover in this project is based on quantity relationships and
   should not be interpreted as a full accounting inventory-turn metric.
6. Business recommendations are based on the available dataset and may need
   operational data for root-cause confirmation.
